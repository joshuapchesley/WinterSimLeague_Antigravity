System Principles

Single Source of Truth

All results originate from one authoritative data store, ensuring consistency across the entire system.

Intentional Handicaps

Suggested handicaps are calculated automatically, but updates are deliberate and transparent to everyone.

Historical Integrity

Past results are never retroactively altered. Handicap changes only affect future rounds and standings.
