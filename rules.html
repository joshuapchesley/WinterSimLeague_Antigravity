League Rules & Information

How the Winter Golf League system works

🏆 Scoring System

The league uses a cumulative point system to determine season standings.

Weekly Points (The Ladder)

Points are awarded based on Net Score ranking each week:

  • 1st Place: 5 points
  • 2nd Place: 4 points
  • 3rd Place: 3 points
  • 4th Place: 2 points
  • 5th Place: 1 point
Tie Breaker: If players/teams tie for a position, the points are split.
Example: If two teams tie for 1st, they split the points for 1st and 2nd (5 + 4 = 9 / 2 = 4.5 points each).

🤝 Formats & Handicaps

Different formats use different handicap calculations to ensure fair play.

Team Scramble

In a Scramble, all players tee off, pick the best shot, and play from there. This continues until the ball is holed.

Team Handicap Calculation:

  • 2-Person Team: 35% of Low Handicap + 15% of High Handicap
  • 3-Person Team: 30% Low + 20% Mid + 10% High
  • 4-Person Team: 25% A + 20% B + 15% C + 10% D

Individual Stroke Play

Standard golf. Count every stroke.

  • Handicap: 100% of Course Handicap is used.

📉 Player Handicaps

Handicaps are calculated dynamically based on your performance.

Establishment & Calculation

  • Establishing a Handicap: A player must complete at least 3 individual stroke play rounds to establish an initial handicap. Once the 3rd round is recorded, the system automatically creates your first official Handicap Index.
  • Updating: After establishment, your "Suggested Handicap" is recalculated every time you play an individual stroke play round. You (or the league admin) can then choose to "Update" your official index to match the suggestion.
  • Calculation Formula:
    1. We calculate a **Handicap Differential** for each round: (Score - Course Rating) × 113 / Slope Rating.
    2. We take the best (lowest) differentials from your history based on the table below.
    3. We average those best differentials and multiply by **0.96** (the USGA "Bonus for Excellence").

Rounds Used Table

7 - 8 3
9 - 10 4

13 - 14 6

20+ 11
Example Calculation (Player with 6 rounds):

Rounds: 85 (13.0), 82 (10.0), 88 (16.0), 84 (12.0), 81 (9.0), 83 (11.0).

According to the table, at 6 rounds we use the best 2 differentials (9.0 and 10.0). Note that once this player adds their 7th round, the system will begin averaging the best 3.

Calculation: (9.0 + 10.0) / 2 = 9.5
9.5 × 0.96 = 9.1 Handicap Index

9-Hole Play Example:

When playing 9 holes, the system handles the normalization automatically:

  • Strokes Received: If your Course Handicap is 10 for 18 holes, you receive 5 strokes for a 9-hole round.
  • Handicap Index: If you shoot a 41 on a Par 36 course, your 9-hole differential is 5.0. This is doubled (10.0) to normalize it to an 18-hole equivalent before it's used in your Handicap Index calculation.

Substitutes

Substitutes can play in any week. Their scores count for the weekly competition (e.g., helping a team win), but they do not accrue season points unless they become active league members.
