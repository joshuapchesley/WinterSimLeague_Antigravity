League Rules & Information

How the Winter Golf League system works

Scoring System

The league uses a cumulative point system to determine season standings.

Weekly Points (The Ladder)

Points are awarded based on Net Score ranking each week:

Tie Breaker: If players/teams tie for a position, the points are split equally.
Example: Two teams tie for 1st place split points: (5 + 4) ÷ 2 = 4.5 points each

Formats & Handicaps

Different formats use different handicap calculations to ensure fair play.

Team Scramble

All players tee off, select the best shot, and play from there until the ball is holed.

Team Handicap Calculation:

  • 2-Person Team: 35% Low + 15% High
  • 3-Person Team: 30% Low + 20% Mid + 10% High
  • 4-Person Team: 25% A + 20% B + 15% C + 10% D

Individual Stroke Play

Standard golf. Count every stroke.

  • Handicap Applied: 100% of Course Handicap

Player Handicaps

Handicaps are calculated dynamically based on your performance in stroke play rounds.

Establishment & Calculation

  • Establishing a Handicap: After your 3rd stroke play round, your first official Handicap Index is automatically created.
  • Updating: Your "Suggested Handicap" recalculates every time you add a round. Choose when to officially update.
  • Formula: We calculate two suggestions (USGA Method and All Rounds Average). The USGA method uses the best differentials from your recent history and applies the 0.96 factor.
  • Transparency: The Handicaps page shows the exact "Pool" of scores, the dates, and identifies which rounds were paired vs 18-hole.

Example Calculation (6 rounds):

Rounds: 85 (13.0), 82 (10.0), 88 (16.0), 84 (12.0), 81 (9.0), 83 (11.0)

With 6 rounds, use the best 2 differentials: (9.0 + 10.0) ÷ 2 = 9.5 × 0.96 = 9.1 Handicap Index

9-Hole Play (GHIN Style): 9-hole rounds are no longer "doubled." Instead, we use GHIN-style pairing. The system takes your 9-hole rounds (oldest first) and pairs them together to form a synthetic 18-hole equivalent. Only completed 18-hole equivalents are used in the differential pool for calculations. Any unpaired 9-hole round sits as "pending" until your next 9.

Substitute Policy

A substitute player is not an active league member and does not earn or modify a handicap. When a substitute is used, the team must select: the player being replaced, and whether the team handicap uses: Proxy: the replaced player’s current handicap, or Manual: the substitute’s commissioner-approved sub handicap. The selected handicap applies only to that week and is recorded for transparency. Sub participation does not affect season handicaps, standings, or future weeks.
