Understanding the Handicap System

Column Definitions:

  • Current: The active index stored in the player profile. This is the ONLY number used for scoring weeks.
  • Suggested (USGA): Calculated using the official USGA/GHIN method. Uses the BEST differentials from the most recent 20 rounds, applies the 0.96 multiplier, and follows GHIN 9-hole pairing rules.
  • Pool: The total number of effective 18-hole rounds available after pairing 9-hole scores. Any single unpaired score is marked as "pending."
  • Transparency: Below each suggestion, the specific differentials, dates, and "paired" tags are shown for full verification.

Calculation Rules (GHIN-Style):

  • 9-Hole Pairing: Two 9-hole rounds are combined into one 18-hole equivalent based on date (oldest first). The date of the 18-hole equivalent is the date of the second (most recent) 9-hole round in the pair.
  • Differentials: Calculated as (Score - Rating) × 113 ÷ Slope.
  • Best Rounds (USGA Only): Only the lowest differentials from the most recent 20 are used, per the USGA index table.

Note: The Current index is never auto-updated. The commissioner must manually review suggestions and click "Update" to apply them.
